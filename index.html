Security and resilience answer different questions

Dimension Traditional security Cyber resilience
Core question Can we keep attackers out? How fast can we keep operating when they get in?
Measured by Threats blocked, tools deployed Time to detect, contain and recover; business impact avoided
Owned by The IT or security team The board, leadership and every business function
Scope Point products for individual risks One programme from governance through recovery

The questions your board will ask after an incident

  • How did they get in, and are they still inside?
  • What data left the building, and whose was it?
  • How long were customers unable to reach us?
  • Did we notify regulators within the deadline?

A resilience programme makes sure you have answers before you are asked.

Questions we hear often

Something else on your mind? Ask a specialist.

What is cyber resilience?

It is your organisation's ability to keep delivering critical services during and after a cyber incident. It covers preventing attacks, but also detecting them quickly, limiting the damage and recovering in a planned way.

How is this different from a managed security service?

A managed security service usually focuses on monitoring and alerts. A resilience programme also covers governance, testing, response and recovery, with one team accountable for the outcome.

Do we have to replace our existing security tools?

No. We start from what you already run, integrate it where possible and only recommend changes where there is a clear gap.

Can we start with a single service?

Yes. Many clients begin with an assessment or a penetration test and extend into monitoring and response once they see the findings.

Which regulations do you help with?

Common ones include CERT-In Directions, SEBI's cyber resilience framework, RBI guidelines, the DPDP Act and ISO 27001. We map controls and evidence to whichever apply to you.

What happens if we are attacked right now?

Call the 24×7 incident hotline listed in the header and footer. A responder will help you contain the incident and preserve evidence, whether or not you are an existing client.

Is this suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Pre-configured packages give smaller teams the same lifecycle coverage without hiring in-house security staff.

How do we get started?

Book a resilience review. A specialist walks through your current setup, and you receive a short written summary of priorities, whether or not you go ahead.
